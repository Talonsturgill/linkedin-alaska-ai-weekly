**Chokepoint.** Layer 5. The FCC Wireline Competition Bureau's Telecommunications Access
Policy Division. Binary: extend the previously-approved-rate waiver to funding year 2028
and beyond, or let it lapse and force fresh Method 3 cost studies. Asymmetric because
paragraph 47 of the Order delegates the FY2028-and-beyond decision to the Bureau alone,
and the FY2026 waiver was already issued by that Bureau on its own authority
(40 FCC Rcd 8795 (WCB 2025)). The 6 August Commission vote is the visible event; the
Bureau holds the durable leverage.

**Structural read.** A rate-setting mechanism wearing a subsidy mechanism's clothes.
Section 254(h)(1)(A) presumes a functioning comparable-rate market and Alaska does not
have one, which is why the Method 3 "safety valve" cost study became the default here and
nowhere else (285 of 317 Alaska Telecom Program requests in FY2024, 306 of 340 in FY2025).
Three consecutive funding years of freezing rates by waiver rather than fixing the
methodology has converted a stopgap into Alaska's de facto rate regime.

**Forward implication.** Before the FY2027 Telecom filing cycle opens, inventory sites
against the waiver's actual scope. Paragraph 48 limits reuse to a prior funding commitment
for the same facility/service combination from FY2024-FY2026. A clinic stepping from
100 Mbps to 1 Gbps has no such predicate and still needs a full Method 3 cost study.
USAC FY2027 webinars: HCF 5 August 2026, Telecom 26 August 2026. FNPRM comments close
30 days after Federal Register publication.
