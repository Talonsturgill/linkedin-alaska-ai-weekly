## Desk position — MEDIOCRE (the messenger, not the message)
Comment triage itself is ordinary and defensible in a large land-use review, and Boyle
did commit that substantive comments "will be addressed in due course." The problem is
who performed the triage. Sorting public opposition into substantive and non-substantive
piles is the referee's job, and Alaska's immediate past DNR Commissioner grading the
opposition to his own company's application in public, mid-review, hands critics a
process argument that costs Stak more than the comments themselves ever would.
Steelman the sharp side, land on mediocre. Do not manufacture a scandal.
