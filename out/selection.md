# Selection — Received Wisdom NO. 22 — 2026-06-21

## The claim (verbatim, attributed)
"Our AI, machine learning, and intelligent automation solutions deliver measurable impact—one current solution alone saves the Army more than $37 million annually"
— **Ron Unger**, Chairman and CEO, Koniag (Alaska Native Regional Corporation), Alaska Business Magazine Regional Roundup, 1 Dec 2025.

## Steelman (the strongest honest version)
A named CEO of an 8(a)-eligible Alaska Native Corporation, whose subsidiary OrangeSlices AI holds active DoD AI/ML contracts, puts a specific, large dollar figure against a named customer. It is not vibes. Koniag Government Services built the DORA bot, an automated vendor-eligibility tool deployed across Army procurement, and a published AWS case study carries the same $37M number. To a reasonable reader this looks like hard proof that an ANC can deliver AI to a federal customer, not just pass the work through to a Lower 48 subcontractor.

## One-sentence corrective thesis
The $37 million is cost avoidance (labor-hours not spent), not realized budget savings, and for federal auditors and appropriators that distinction changes what the number is worth.

## Evidence spine (all in claim_dossier.json, re-verified)
- AWS case study (primary, directly fetched) labels the figure "annual cost avoidance" / "yearly avoidance," not savings. Mechanism: 687,000 labor-hours saved per year via DORA. Koniag's own VP of AI Strategy, Carin Chapman: the tool "doesn't replace the professional. It gives them back nearly an hour each time."
- Cost avoidance leaves the Army's appropriation unchanged; savings reduce actual outlays. GAO treats them as categorically different.

## AK industry consequence
ANC 8(a) federal AI contracting. Koniag and OrangeSlices AI compete for DoD AI/ML primes under the ANC 8(a) lane, which carries no revenue cap and permits sole-source awards above the small-business ceiling. A "$37M savings" framing taken at face value inflates the perceived ROI of ANC-prime AI work, can pull more sole-source task orders on a false premise of auditable budget impact, and parks reputational and recompete risk on the ANC when auditors apply cost-avoidance standards and find no realized savings. The cost rides on the broader ANC-as-AI-prime narrative Alaska economic-development advocates are building right now.

## Mode
Corrective Explainer. Posture is generous: the capability is real and impressive; the accounting word is the load-bearing error.
