# VOL. 01 Selection — 2026-05-12

## Mode
**Weekly Brief** — four converging in-window decisions across power, statute, workforce, and capital, with one counter-momentum story. Too many real news hooks this week to commit to a single Deep Dive without leaving live deadlines on the table.

## Package angle
Three different non-engineering gates are closing on Alaska's AI buildout in the next 8 to 17 days. The decisive variables this week aren't models or chips. They're gas, statute, and the local talent bench. A fourth gate, the UAF-DIU MOU, opened slightly in the same week. The week reads as a stress test of whether Alaska's institutional plumbing can keep pace with the federal AI infrastructure push that's now physically pointed at JBER, Eielson, and Clear.

- Confidence: high. Primary source: af.mil. In-window: ADN May 5.
