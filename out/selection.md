# Anchorage Desk — selection — 2026-09-17

- **Subject:** Randy Ruaro
- **Role:** Executive Director, Alaska Industrial Development and Export Authority (AIDEA)
- **Org:** AIDEA, the State of Alaska's development-finance authority, headquartered at 813 W Northern Lights Blvd, Anchorage
- **Role category / kicker slot:** municipal → `MUNICIPAL`
- **Decision:** Pursuing, and defending before DNR through the public-comment period that closed Sept 14, 2026, a noncompetitive, no-cost conveyance of nearly 20,000 acres (approx. 30 square miles per Alaska Beacon; "over 31 square miles" per KTUU) of state land north of Houston to AIDEA for a "multi-use industrial and energy development district" whose outlined uses include "development of scalable data centers that leverage existing and planned utility corridors." In a letter to DNR answering a bipartisan legal challenge from five state senators (letters Aug 19 and Sept 9), Ruaro wrote: "Let me be clear. There is no data center project planned, nor has one ever been discussed with a project proponent."

- **Structural read:** A defensible bet on capturing compute investment, but the mechanism (no-cost, noncompetitive, no named tenant, no project-level DNR review) is the whole argument. Ruaro's "no data center project planned" defuses the giveaway charge and simultaneously validates the senators' point that DNR is conveying land for uses it cannot yet evaluate. The load-bearing tension is Railbelt energy scarcity.
