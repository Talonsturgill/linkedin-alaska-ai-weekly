## Mechanism

185(n) mandatory-renewal floor against the 185(f) conditioning power. BLM NEPA Number
**DOI-BLM-AK-9410-2026-0009-EA**.

> "The U.S. Bureau of Land Management has kicked off the process to renew the
> right-of-way agreement for the pipeline."

Coverage framed this as an environmental-shortcut story (a less-comprehensive review

## Layers (5)
1. **The mandatory-renewal floor, 30 U.S.C. 185(n)** — caps the grant at thirty years and
   directs that the agency head *shall renew* while the project is in commercial
   operation. Secretary of the Interior / agency head.
2. **The conditioning power, 30 U.S.C. 185(f)** — open-ended authority over extent,
   duration, survey, location, construction, operation, maintenance, use and
   termination. No limiting standard. Same authority, exercised through BLM Alaska.
3. **BLM Alaska renewal docket and the NEPA instrument** — DOI-BLM-AK-9410-2026-0009-EA,
   scoping 26 Aug through 10 Sep 2026. **Kevin Pendergast, BLM Alaska State Director.**
4. **The grantee stack** — Alyeska Pipeline Service Company as applicant and operator, on
   behalf of Harvest Alaska LLC, ConocoPhillips Transportation Alaska Inc. and
   ExxonMobil Pipeline Company LLC.
5. **The parallel state corridor gate** — AS 38.35.070, ROW lease ADL 63574. Four riprap
   stockpile areas, ~26.1 acres, mileposts 77.5–82.4. **Anthony Strupulis, P.E., State
   Pipeline Coordinator.** Objections due 21 Sep 2026, 5:00 p.m.

## Chokepoint
Layer 3. Kevin Pendergast, BLM Alaska State Director, owns one binary: build the
thirty-year renewal record on an environmental assessment closing scoping at fifteen
days on 10 September, or elevate it to an environmental impact statement. 185(n) takes
"whether" off the table; 185(f) puts all of "on what terms" in one office. No committee
vote, no rate case, no legislative concurrence.

## Structural read
The machinery produces a thirty-year land-use rulebook while looking like a routine
permit renewal, and the statutory design is what makes that possible. Congress
guaranteed the renewal and left every term unguarded. The loud question is already
answered and takes all the attention; the question that allocates value for a generation
is being settled on a fifteen-day window with almost nobody at the table.

## Forward implication
Two dates, six and seventeen days out. Federal scoping comment by 10 Sep 2026 — the only
step that builds a record before the stipulations exist. State objection at ADL 63574 by
5:00 p.m. 21 Sep 2026. Alyeska and the three owners are positioned in both forums.
Everyone else is positioned in neither unless they file this week.
