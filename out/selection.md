## Package angle
This week Alaska kept selling cheap power it has not secured. The Air Force's lease solicitation for AI data centers at JBER, Eielson, and Clear closes June 29, and some of those parcels would pull from a Fairbanks grid burning diesel near a record fuel cost. The one bill that would fix the math, the LNG pipeline tax break that the developer says it needs to reach a final investment decision, passed the House 34-5 on June 12 and then stalled in the Senate with the special session expiring June 19. The same day the Senate sat on it, FERC moved to fast-track AI data-center hookups across the Lower 48 and to make those loads pay for their own grid upgrades, a principle Alaska has not adopted. Meanwhile the AI itself keeps arriving, an ML cytobot is scanning the Bering Sea for toxic algae right now. The through-line for the LinkedIn audience is that Alaska is underwriting a hyperscale buildout on a power price the legislature failed to deliver, and the question of who absorbs that cost, the developer, the base, or the Fairbanks ratepayer, is being decided in the next three weeks on deadlines the state does not control.
