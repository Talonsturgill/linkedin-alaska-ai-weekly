# Received Wisdom NO. 07 — Selection

## The claim (verbatim)
> "Many small boat fishermen prefer EM systems over human observers, so increasing the usefulness and effectiveness of EM should have direct benefits to our members and Alaska's fixed gear fleet in general."

**Asserter:** Lauren Howard, Policy Coordinator, Alaska Longline Fishermen's Association (ALFA), via National Fisherman, 1 Aug 2025.
**Slice:** exec_social. **Circulation:** ~5 outlets (National Fisherman, SeafoodSource, Cordova Times, Alaska's News Source, ALFA).

## Steelman (the strongest honest version)
Small fixed-gear and halibut vessels have real economic and privacy reasons to prefer electronic monitoring over carrying a human observer: no extra bunk, no insurance rider, no interpersonal friction. A 2023 Ai.Fish field study found ~48% time and ~46% cost savings per haul review when AI assistance was added, and NOAA already lets fixed-gear vessels opt into the EM pool in lieu of observer coverage. Combine genuine fleet preference with tangible efficiency gains and AI-EM looks like the better mousetrap, ready to scale.

## One-sentence corrective thesis
Fleet preference for EM is real and rational, but it is a demand-side fact that does not establish the supply-side claim hiding inside it, that AI-assisted EM is yet a validated, observer-equivalent substitute, because the best field evidence shows AI roughly doubles the human-review error rate, still needs a human verifying every haul, and degrades under routine camera occlusion.

## Evidence spine (primary, re-verified)
1. **Ai.Fish / PSMFC 2023 field study** — AI-assisted review mean error rate 2.7% vs 1.3% human-only (roughly double); humans verified results haul-by-haul; "significantly poorer" under camera occlusion. AI is a supplement, not a substitute. (em4.fish)
2. **NOAA Fisheries Alaska EM page** (current) — no mention of AI-assisted review; EM described as a compliance tool working alongside, not replacing, observers; fixed-gear EM is a voluntary annual opt-in. (fisheries.noaa.gov)

## Alaska industry consequence
The North Pacific Fishery Management Council's 2026 Annual Deployment Plan and NOAA observer-program budget decisions for the ~$2.7B Alaska groundfish and halibut sectors turn on whether AI-EM is treated as a validated observer alternative or an efficiency layer pending validation. Vendors, insurers, and fleet operators pricing observer-cost relief into 2026-2027 models may be ahead of the evidence.
