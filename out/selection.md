## Chokepoint

The authority is non-delegable under 15 U.S.C. § 637(a). Zero new applications approved since August 2025, versus approximately 600 in 2024. No court order, state resolution, or congressional mandate (short of statutory amendment) can compel the Administrator to act on any timeline.

## Structural Read
The 8(a) ANC machine is a three-decade-old revenue amplifier that converts ANCSA land-settlement legitimacy into federal contract access, with the SBA Administrator as a single administrative valve. The current freeze is not legal abolition — the statute is intact and existing certified firms continue receiving awards — but it is a slow suffocation of the subsidiary pipeline. For ANC-owned AI and technology contractors (Arctic IT/Doyon, Koniag Government Services, Chugach Government Solutions), the freeze means they cannot spin up new 8(a) subsidiaries to capture the wave of federal AI spending at precisely the moment when the contracting opportunity is largest.

Murkowski's office on whether a statutory fix or appropriations rider can impose processing timelines on the SBA Administrator — the only lever that does not require the Administrator's voluntary cooperation.
