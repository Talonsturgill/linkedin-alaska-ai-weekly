## Decision
On April 22, 2026, GCI Holdings, LLC entered a definitive agreement to
acquire 100% of Q Gateway Intermediate Holdings, LLC (Quintillion), a fiber
infrastructure provider, for $310 million enterprise value, plus up to $50
million capex reimbursement for Quintillion's Nome-to-Homer Express project
and potential earnout payments in 2028, 2029, and 2031. GCI also provided a
$160 million unsecured loan to Quintillion. The combined network will span
3,316 route miles of subsea and terrestrial fiber, forming a self-healing
ringed architecture that eliminates the outage vulnerability that knocked
Quintillion's cable offline twice in three years (2023, 2025). Ron Duncan,
as CEO, authorized and signed the acquisition, consolidating Alaska's two
largest private fiber networks under single GCI ownership. Closing is
pending regulatory approval.

largest fiber networks eliminates a catastrophic single-point-of-failure

leases.
